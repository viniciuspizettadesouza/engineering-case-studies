# Privacy and Intellectual Property Policy

## Purpose

This repository demonstrates current engineering judgement using original, fictional implementations. It must not function as a reconstruction or disclosure of a former employer's product.

## Never include

- source code written for or owned by an employer or client;
- screenshots, recordings or visual replicas of private products;
- employer or client brand assets;
- internal tickets, documents, diagrams, prompts or messages;
- real customer, employee or operational data;
- private API contracts, payloads, schemas or infrastructure details;
- business rules learned only through confidential access;
- credentials, secrets, tokens or production endpoints;
- fabricated claims presented as real business outcomes.

## Safe sources

- original code written specifically for this repository;
- public web standards and official technology documentation;
- broadly known domain concepts;
- invented organisations, identities, data and metrics;
- Vinicius's public résumé-level descriptions of his roles;
- architectural reasoning derived independently for the fictional problem.

## Naming and visual identity

Every case-study organisation must have an invented name and neutral visual system. Names, logos, colours, layouts and copy should not create the impression that a previous employer endorsed or supplied the work.

## Data policy

Fixtures must be obviously fictional and contain no real personal data. Avoid realistic combinations of names, addresses, account numbers and contact details when they are not required. Secrets and private environment variables are prohibited in the static application.

## Claims policy

Case-study metrics are labelled as budgets, targets or simulated results. Real career outcomes may be quoted only when already public and clearly separated from results produced by the fictional application.

## Review checklist

Before merging a study, confirm:

- Could this code have been written without access to a former employer's repository?
- Are all organisations, users and workflows independently invented?
- Does any visual or written material resemble a private product too closely?
- Are simulated metrics and statuses labelled honestly?
- Does the public page contain the fictional-content disclaimer?
- Do the staged files contain credentials or personal information?

If an answer creates doubt, replace the material with a more generic, independently designed alternative before publication.
